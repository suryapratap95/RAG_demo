# Payments FAQ

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Cash on delivery is not available.

## Instalments
Orders over 200 dollars can be paid in 4 interest-free instalments through our pay-later partner. The first instalment is charged at checkout.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order is held for 24 hours so you can try another card. After 24 hours the order is cancelled automatically.

## Invoices
An invoice is emailed with the order confirmation. Business customers can add a company name and tax ID at checkout.
