# Return Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Opened software, gift cards and personal care items cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Log in to your account, open the order, and choose "Return item". We email you a prepaid shipping label within one business day. Drop the parcel at any partner courier location.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## Exchanges
If you want a different size or colour, return the original item and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the replacement.
