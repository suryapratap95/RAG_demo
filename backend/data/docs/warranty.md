# Warranty

## Coverage
All electronics come with a one-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage or normal wear.

## Extended warranty
You can buy a two-year extended warranty at checkout for 15 percent of the product price. Extended warranty also covers one accidental damage repair.

## Making a claim
Contact support with your order number and a short description or photo of the fault. If the claim is approved we repair or replace the product within 10 business days. If the product can no longer be repaired, we issue store credit.
