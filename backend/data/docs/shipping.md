# Shipping Information

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars. Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day.

## Free shipping
Orders over 50 dollars get free standard shipping. Express shipping is never free.

## International orders
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties are paid by the customer on arrival.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. You can also see tracking under "My orders" in your account.
